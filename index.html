How it works

From first conversation to app on the store

  1. Conversation and scopeI work out what the app needs to do and write down what is in and what is out. You approve it before any code exists.
  2. Fixed-price proposalOne page: scope, timeline, price and payment terms. Half on signature, half on delivery.
  3. Build, with you watchingA running view of what is done and what is left, without having to ask.
  4. Testing before releaseA documented manual sweep plus automated tests. A defect found here costs minutes; found by your customer, it costs your store rating.
  5. Publishing on both storesAccounts, signing, icons, listings, privacy policy, data safety declaration and the required testing process.
  6. Handover and maintenanceCode and documentation are yours. Monthly maintenance is a separate contract, and I explain why it is usually needed.

Something almost nobody mentions upfront: the stores break apps that sit still. Android and iOS raise their minimum requirements every year, and an app that does not keep up gets removed. That is part of the conversation from the start, not a surprise two years later.
